## Why this site

- **18 rodent reports in 2025–2026**, each closed within days, then reported again.
- **The city escalated every step and the rats came back anyway**: monitoring → order to clear overgrown foliage → weeds citation → code enforcement violation → ticket → abatement order → violation sent to collections → violation to the owners of the adjacent lot.
- **The likely root cause is next door**: the adjacent parcel is classed "RES LAND (Unusable)", a vacant lot.
