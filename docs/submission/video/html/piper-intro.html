Soot, bone, brass, vermilion.

Warm means it happened. Blue means it might. Brass means you can act. Components use semantic tokens only. Contrast is computed live against the current theme.

03 · Typography

A masthead serif, a hyperlegible sans.

Bodoni Moda for one headline per panel, 28 px and up. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for everything you read and click. Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono for every record.

04 · Space, size, shape

Four-pixel steps, few radii.

Cards pad 16, panels pad 20, cards sit 12 apart, sections 24. Controls are 32, 40 or 48 tall. Nothing interactive is smaller than 24 × 24.

08 · Standards

Where the rules live.

This page is a picture. The rules are in docs/design/. Token values come from docs/design/tokens/tokens.json and scripts/check-design-tokens.py keeps the code copies honest.

Do
  • Use semantic tokens (fg-2, return), never raw hex.
  • Pair every status color with a shape and a word.
  • Put a mono source line under every number.
  • One Bodoni line per panel, 28 px or larger.
  • Let motion end. Only the Live dot loops.
Don't
  • Draw a cartoon rat, a photo, or a sewer green.
  • Call a place “infested” or shade a neighborhood red.
  • Use brass for data or vermilion for buttons.
  • Hide focus under glass or require a drag.
  • Invent a value that is not in tokens.json.
