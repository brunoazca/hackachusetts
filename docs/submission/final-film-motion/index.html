What these are

Three silent 1920 × 1080, 30 fps clips cut into a 2:58 film: the opening at 0:00 (420 frames), the context bridge at 1:08 (150 frames) and the closing at 2:50 (240 frames). Motion is spring-driven (closed-form damped oscillator), every visible value is derived from the clip-local time, and the page exposes window.piperMotion (ready, selectClip, seek, play, pause, restart, setReducedMotion).

URL parameters: ?clip=opening|bridge|closing, &t=4.2 loads paused at that time, &capture=1 renders the clean 1920 × 1080 frame with full motion regardless of the OS reduced-motion setting. Without capture, a reduced-motion preference shows the film as still scenes.

  • Opening. “Closed.” A report mark, a recorded closure on one line. “Reported again.” — a later report with a coral ring. The line turns into a piper's pipe before a storybook town; three rat silhouettes follow it (“A legend.”). The town becomes an abstract street grid and the pipe becomes the line that groups records around one place (“A new purpose.”). The line resolves into the pipe of the Piper logo. “Evidence for the next inspection.”

  • Closing. The place marker and a short line; the line becomes the logo pipe; “Piper”, “Follow the rats.”, “Evidence for the next inspection.” Pixel-still for the last four seconds.
