# Product — Hackachusetts 2026 (Civic Action track)

## What we are building
**Piper (Rat Audit Boston)**: an evidence-review tool for repeated rodent reports.
An inspector starts with Places to visit, opens an address, reviews reports after
closure and recorded responses, and examines nearby records. The map and report
forecast add context; they do not establish causes or treatment effectiveness.
Recommendations, approval/work-order flow, assistant, and trial design remain
planned until their complete paths work. Do not describe them as shipped.
Spec: `.kiro/specs/rat-audit/`.

## Who it is for
ISD rodent inspectors and the Boston Rodent Action Plan coordinator (primary);
residents and councillors (read-only).

## Non-negotiables (from the rules)
- Public GitHub repo with a root `/.kiro` directory holding real work.
- Working project, English description, public demo video under 3 minutes.
- A specific Kiro usage write-up. Meaningful `.kiro` usage or risk of disqualification.
- Submit on SundAI by 8:00 PM Saturday, October 24. Start at ~6:00 PM.
- Judges may score from the description, images, and video alone. The
  submission text must explain what works without a live demo.

## What "good" looks like
- Authoritative data source cited in-product (not an unsupported claim).
- Accessible UI: keyboard reachable, readable contrast, screen-reader labels.
- A visible human decision point, not full automation of a civic choice.
- Scope one service well over many services shallowly.

## Out of scope
- Claims of citywide transformation.
- Any civic decision made without a human in the loop.
- Collecting real personal data beyond what the demo needs.
